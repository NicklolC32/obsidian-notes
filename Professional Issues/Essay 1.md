**"How might your work on this project contribute to unintentional harm?**

- data analysis Report of Housing Crisis in Edinburgh
#### Potential Harms:
- collecting data through one survey which is available to the public and aimed towards students from Edinburgh - survey allows anonymity (protects personal privacy, ACM 1.6 and 1.7) however there is risk of innacuracy because this may be a reason for people to lie on the survey or answer even if they are not part of the audience the report is based on
	- therefore, could be a misleading report because data visualisation might not be accurate or unclear
	- social impact: since survey is open to the public -> questions how reliable this report truly is and whether our conclusions can be trusted or used
	- graphs need to avoid portraying the wrong idea or being exaggerated (ACM: 1.3 Be honest and trustworthy)
	
- social impact: landlords could be students who need extra money. This report targets the Edinburgh council as well as landlords, could this negatively affect Edinburgh's landlords' careers? Cost of Living crisis in Edinburgh is also part of the question - what if a landlord needs the money in order to live?
- disclosure/non-disclosure of identity - option to remain anonymous but data can sometimes be retraceable especially if the survey is based on students' living conditions
	- **no guaranteed of full anonymity** option to provide written answers, if we want to quote answers verbatim and the user is happy for us to use their data, there is a trade-off here between accurately quoting what was said for identity non-disclosure [1]
